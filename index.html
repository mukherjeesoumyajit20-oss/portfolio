My story

It started on a sofa in Kolkata.

In 2017 I sat on a sofa in a flat in Kolkata, trying to sell a study plan to a father who had already said no twice.

I stopped pitching and asked what he was actually worried about. It was not the money. It was his elder daughter's board exams, two years away, and whether any of this would still be useful by then.

We built a plan that ran from his younger son's grade 5 all the way to his daughter's class 12. Close to Rs 2.5 lakh. That was my first month at Toppr.

The objection people say out loud is rarely the real one.
OYO · Sell to owners

I signed hotel partners across West Bengal and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. I onboarded over 500 rooms and negotiated revenue share terms directly with owners who ran the numbers better than I did. You cannot charm a hotel owner. You have to show him his own P&L.

Vedantu · Start leading

I carried my own quota, closed Rs 5 to 6 lakh a month in IIT and NEET courses, and trained a team of 4 to 5 BDAs. I also opened a B2B line and signed around 20 schools. Watching a BDA I coached close her first big deal felt better than closing my own.

Accredian · Bigger tickets

Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 4 lakh programs from IIT, IIM and XLRI, sold to people with years of experience who had already been pitched by three competitors. I was promoted twice in under two years and led 6 to 7 consultants.

Scaler · Top seller, then pod lead

My target was 4 to 5 closures a month. For three straight quarters I ran at 8 to 9 and was the top performing BDA on the floor. In February 2026 I became an Associate Manager. I still carried my number and spent most of my week fixing discovery calls, because that is where deals quietly die.

Interview Kickstart · Today

I now run sales for the US-AIML business as Manager Sales. I own revenue, pipeline and forecast. I coach through weekly 1:1s, pipeline reviews and live calls, and I track stage conversion, not just activity.

What I am good at

Getting to the real objection. Holding price without losing the deal. Keeping a pipeline honest enough that the forecast means something. Coaching new sellers so they ramp in weeks, not months.
